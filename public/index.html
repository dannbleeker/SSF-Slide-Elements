✓ SSF Slide Elements

A library of ready-made slide elements for PowerPoint.

Browse the collection in the task pane, click an element, and it lands on the slide you are on — with every font, colour, table and chart exactly as it was drawn.

Installing

Sideload manifest-prod.xml from the repository. It needs PowerPoint with PowerPointApi 1.2 or better — Microsoft 365 on Windows or Mac, PowerPoint on the web, or PowerPoint 2021.

Privacy

The add-in sends nothing anywhere. The element library is served with the add-in from this domain and your presentation is read and written entirely inside PowerPoint on your own machine. There is no account, no telemetry and no server that sees your slides.
