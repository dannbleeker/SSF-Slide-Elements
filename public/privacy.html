Privacy policy

SSF Slide Elements · last updated 11 September 2026

SSF Slide Elements does not collect, store, transmit or share any of your data. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Everything it does happens inside the PowerPoint task pane, on your own device.

Who provides SSF Slide Elements

SSF Slide Elements is made and published by DBP Invest ApS, a company registered in Denmark, under the publisher name StruktureretSundFornuft. Where this policy says “we”, that is who it means.

What SSF Slide Elements reads

SSF Slide Elements is a library of ready-made slide elements for Microsoft PowerPoint. To do its work it will read one thing:

  • The presentation you have open, so it can put an element onto the slide you are on.

The element library itself ships inside the add-in as static files. Your presentation is processed in the add-in's own pane, on your device, and is not sent anywhere. The changed slide is written straight back into the presentation you already have open, through Microsoft's own Office JavaScript interface.

What we collect

Nothing.

We operate no server that receives your content. The add-in makes no network requests of any kind while it runs. There is no sign-in, no account, no licence check and no usage tracking. We could not identify you, or a presentation you have used it on, if we were asked to.

What is stored on your device

Four things, so the pane opens where you left it. In your browser's local storage, under the key ssf-slide-elements: which insert target and grouping you chose behind the gear, which elements you have starred, the last six you inserted, which categories you left open, and whether you have dismissed the getting-started note.

That is a list of our own element names and two settings. It holds nothing from your presentation, nothing about you, and it never leaves the device it was written on — there is nowhere for it to go, because the add-in sends nothing anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it, and the pane opens perfectly well without it.

This section was wrong until 11 September 2026: it said nothing was stored, and had said so since before the picker was built. The claim is now checked by a test that reads this page against the code, so it cannot go stale again without the build failing.

Third parties

We send your data to no third party, because we send it nowhere at all.

The add-in's own files — the pane and its icons — are served over HTTPS from ssf-slide-elements.struktureretsundfornuft.dk, hosted on GitHub Pages. The add-in also loads Microsoft's office.js library from Microsoft's content delivery network, as every Office add-in is required to. Your browser contacting those hosts to fetch those files is a request like any other on the web, and their own privacy practices apply to it. Neither request carries any of your presentation content.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct or delete.

If you want SSF Slide Elements to leave no trace on your device, remove the add-in in PowerPoint.

Children

SSF Slide Elements is a business productivity tool and is not directed at children.
